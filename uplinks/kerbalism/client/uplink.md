Reads a [Kerbalism](https://github.com/Kerbalism/Kerbalism) save the way the mod
itself models one: every profile resource as a meter with the per-source rates
that move it, plus habitat, processes, greenhouses, wear and the crew rules that
run a Kerbal down. Kerbalism must be installed for any of it to appear; without
it the Uplink loads and reports nothing rather than failing, because every
Kerbalism member is reached by reflection.

Most of what it adds is not a widget of its own. Radiation and survival ride
into the base Crew Status widget, part meters and wear ride into Ship Map, and
the CME warning rides into System View, so a Kerbalism fact sits beside the
stock fact it qualifies.
