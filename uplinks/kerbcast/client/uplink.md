Shows live views from the cameras on the active craft, such as Hullcam VDS parts and crew faces, on the dashboard. kerbcast must be installed in the same KSP.

An operator picks which camera to watch and can aim and zoom the ones that support it. Aim and zoom are commands, so they take the signal delay like any other; the video itself is a separate live stream.

It also fills two slots in widgets the app already draws: a close-range docking camera behind the Targeting widget, and a live face for each Kerbal in Crew Status.
