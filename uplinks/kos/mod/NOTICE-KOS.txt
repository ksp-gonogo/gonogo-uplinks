GonogoKosUplink compile-time links kOS (kOS.dll / kOS.Safe.dll) and reads it
in-process: kOS's public API, plus one private field of kOS's processor module
(the CPU's shared objects), read by reflection. No loopback telnet.

GonogoKosUplink direct-links kOS, GonogoKosUplink is itself GPL-3.0-only.

gonogo as a whole is MIT-licensed (repository root LICENSE). GonogoKosUplink is
the deliberate exception: it is the only assembly in the repository that
links a copyleft dependency, and it is a leaf, nothing references it, so
its GPL obligations propagate to nothing else. The MIT-licensed Sitrep.*
assemblies it links are GPL-compatible in that direction, and GonogoKosUplink
ships as its own CKAN package in its own GameData folder.
