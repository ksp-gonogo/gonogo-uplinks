Brings [Principia](https://github.com/mockingbirdnest/Principia)'s n-body flight
onto the dashboard: the orbit a trajectory actually settles into, the flight plan
read coast by coast, and a burn editor that composes a manœuvre and sends it back
over the delayed command path. Plan slots can be created, duplicated and deleted
from the board, and the plan's end instant and integrator step limit are set from
it too. The reference frame every figure is quoted in, and the integrator settings
behind it, live in Settings rather than in a widget, because they change what every
other number on the screen means.
