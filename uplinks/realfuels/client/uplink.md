Answers the two questions a [RealFuels](https://github.com/KSP-RO/RealFuels) burn
turns on: can this engine be lit again, and is the propellant settled enough to
light it. The ignition budget, the ullage state and the vessel's boiloff rate take
a seat inside the base Fuel & ΔV widget rather than a widget of their own, so they
sit beside the ΔV budget that assumes the burn will happen.
