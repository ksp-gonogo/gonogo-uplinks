Brings [RP-1](https://github.com/KSP-RO/RP-0)'s career layer to the dashboard:
Programs with their objectives, deadlines, funding curves and the Confidence
price at each speed; the launch complexes, the build queue and the tooling a
vehicle still needs; and the astronaut corps with its courses, retirements and
proficiency. Most of it takes a seat inside the space-centre widgets the app
already draws rather than a widget of its own, so an RP-1 career reads as the
same screen with the career's own rules applied to it.
