Brings [SCANsat](https://github.com/S-C-A-N/SCANsat)'s orbital survey data onto the
mission-control dashboard: what has been scanned, by which craft, and where the
anomalies are. SCANsat must be installed in the same KSP.

Beyond its own widget it adds to ones the app already draws: scan coverage and the
footprints of scanning craft on the map, SCANsat's altimetry and biome maps as map
backgrounds, discovered anomalies as map points, and SCANsat's scanners in the
Experiments widget.
