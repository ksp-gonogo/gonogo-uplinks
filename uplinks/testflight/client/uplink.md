Reads [TestFlight](https://github.com/KSP-RO/TestFlight)'s engine reliability for
the active craft: which engines have failed and in what words TestFlight names
the failure, how much of each rated burn is spent, and the chance an engine
survives its next rated burn at the flight data it has earned. TestFlight must be
installed for any of it to appear; without it the Uplink loads and reports
nothing, because every TestFlight member is reached by reflection.

It adds no widget of its own. The engines worth a look ride onto the active
craft's Fleet Roster row, and a failed engine carries TestFlight's own repair.
That repair takes no crew, no item and no time, so it is one confirmed press with
no cost beside it.
